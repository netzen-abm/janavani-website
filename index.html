TRUST BOUNDARIES

What Janavani is not.

Not a Government Authority

Janavani does not exercise governmental authority or issue official decisions.

Not a Replacement

Janavani does not replace legitimate institutions, officials or established civic processes.

Not an AI Authority

Technology should assist understanding without becoming the final authority over civic decisions.

Not Merely a Telegram Bot

The Telegram interface is one access point within a broader civic-infrastructure vision.
