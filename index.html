Laws We Use

Your complaints are backed by Indian law

Constitution of India

Fundamental rights and duties of citizens and the state

Bhartiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)

Evidence Act for documenting your complaint legally

Information Technology Act, 2000

Protection against cyber harassment and digital records

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Rights when government services are treated as public services

Indian Contract Act, 1872

Legal obligations of government agencies to citizens

Right to Information Act, 2005

Your right to ask for information and transparency
