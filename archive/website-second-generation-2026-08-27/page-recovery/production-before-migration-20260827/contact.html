Help Build Janavani

Civic infrastructure grows through participation, research, technology and shared experience.

Janavani is being built as infrastructure for citizen voice. There is more than one way to contribute.

Start With What You Can Do

You do not need to be a developer or legal professional to contribute to Janavani.

A useful contribution may be a civic problem, research, documentation, code, design, communication, verification, feedback or practical experience.

Ways to Participate

Citizens

Share real-world civic problems, experiences and ideas that can help improve citizen workflows.

Civic Volunteers

Help with civic research, documentation, awareness, community participation and practical project support.

Developers

Contribute code, testing, accessibility, security research, architecture and open-source development.

Legal & Civic Researchers

Help improve procedural information, document structures, legal references and civic pathways.

Governance Researchers

Research public administration, institutions, accountability, participation and governance systems.

Designers & Writers

Help make complex civic and government processes easier for ordinary citizens to understand.

Social Media & Content Creators

Help create explainers, civic-awareness content, infographics, short-form communication and campaigns.

Organisations

Civil-society organisations and community groups can explore research, awareness and collaboration.

Citizen Vigilance

A healthy democracy requires more than voting. Citizens can observe, question, document, verify, communicate and follow up.

Janavani aims to make those forms of participation easier to understand and easier to organise.

Observe. Document. Verify. Participate.

Lawful Civic Action

Contributions to Janavani should strengthen informed, lawful and responsible civic participation.

Janavani does not encourage harassment, intimidation, unlawful action or the targeting of individuals.

The objective is better communication between citizens and public institutions.

Protect Your Own Privacy

Do not publish unnecessary personal, financial, identity, authentication or other sensitive information when contributing to public project discussions.

Contributors should independently evaluate what information is appropriate to share publicly.

Privacy is part of Janavani's infrastructure philosophy. Citizen participation should not require unnecessary exposure of personal information.

From Users to Co-Creators

Janavani can become stronger when the people who use it also help improve it.

Your experience can reveal a missing pathway. Your research can improve accuracy. Your code can improve the infrastructure. Your design can make information easier to understand. Your feedback can expose what needs to change.

Meaningful civic infrastructure is built over time, through many useful contributions.

Important Notice

Janavani is a civic-technology initiative. Information provided through the project is intended to support citizen understanding and participation.

Janavani does not replace a lawyer, court, government authority or other competent professional.

Laws, procedures, deadlines and official requirements should be independently verified before taking legal or administrative action.
