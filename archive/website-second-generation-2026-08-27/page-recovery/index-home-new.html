What Is Janavani?

A civic intelligence layer between citizens and public systems.

Citizens should describe the problem. Janavani should carry the complexity of understanding systems, pathways, and relevant information.

The citizen remains the decision-maker. Janavani is designed to reduce procedural friction while preserving citizen agency.

Current Capability

What Janavani is designed to help citizens do.

Understand

Make public systems, responsibilities, processes, and civic pathways easier to understand.

Identify

Work toward identifying relevant offices, authorities, procedures, and next steps using verified information where available.

Prepare

Support structured civic information and documents that citizens can review before acting.

Participate

Support informed civic participation while keeping decisions and agency with the citizen.

Document

Help create clearer records of concerns, pathways, responses, and outcomes.

Learn

Turn individual interactions into opportunities for civic understanding and system learning.

Technology & Citizen Agency

Technology should support the citizen.

Technology can reduce complexity, but it should not replace citizen judgment, responsibility, or lawful decision-making.

Trust & Boundaries

What Janavani is not.

Clear boundaries are part of trustworthy civic infrastructure.

  • Not a Government Authority.
  • Not a replacement for qualified professionals or official decisions.
  • Not an AI authority that decides what a citizen must do.
  • Not merely a Telegram bot; the project is developing as broader civic infrastructure.

Citizen Vigilance

Observe. Document. Verify. Question. Participate.

Better civic participation depends on attention, evidence, follow-through, and feedback.
