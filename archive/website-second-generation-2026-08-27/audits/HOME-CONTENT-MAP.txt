NON-DESTRUCTIVE EDITING RULE
----------------------------
Do not delete substantive existing content merely to shorten the page.

If content is duplicated:
- retain the strongest version;
- consolidate duplicates;
- move supporting detail to the appropriate page.

If content is useful but misplaced:
- relocate it;
- do not discard it.

If content is outdated:
- verify before modifying;
- preserve the original in the backup.
