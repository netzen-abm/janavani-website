Janavani is designed to reduce the practical complexity between a citizen's concern and the next appropriate civic step.

JANAVANI ASSISTS. THE CITIZEN DECIDES.

01 Â· The six-stage journey

A concern becomes a structured civic pathway.

The citizen begins with an ordinary-language concern. Janavani helps make the institutional path easier to understand, while the citizen remains responsible for reviewing and choosing the action.

01

Describe

Explain the concern in your own words. What happened? What is affecting you or your community?

02

Understand

Clarify what kind of public-system issue may be involved and what information is relevant to understanding it.

03

Identify

Work toward identifying the relevant authority, office, jurisdiction, procedure, or possible civic pathway where reliable information is available.

04

Prepare

Organise the necessary information and prepare an appropriate document, representation, or other civic material for review.

05

Act

Review the result and take the appropriate action through the relevant official or civic channel. Janavani does not make that decision for you.

06

Follow

Keep records, understand responses, observe outcomes, and continue lawful civic participation where appropriate.

06 Â· Information discipline

Not every answer has the same status.

A trustworthy civic system should distinguish verified information, citizen-provided information, assistance, and uncertainty.

VERIFIED

Known from reliable information

Structured sources or implementation evidence support the claim. Verification status should remain visible where it matters.

ASSISTANCE

AI or system interpretation

Generated or interpreted material can help organise a problem, but it is not automatically an official determination.

UNCERTAIN

Information needs confirmation

When reliable information is unavailable or ambiguous, Janavani should expose the uncertainty and provide an appropriate manual path.

07 Â· Trust boundary

Assistance is not authority.

The workflow exists to improve citizen understanding and agency. It should never silently turn automation into institutional authority.

NOTA government department or official decision-maker.
NOTA court, legal authority, or substitute for qualified professional advice.
NOTAn AI oracle that decides what a citizen must do.
NOTA guarantee that an office will respond or that a problem will be resolved.
YESA civic assistance layer designed to make public pathways easier to understand and navigate.
