What Is Janavani?

A civic intelligence layer between citizens and public systems.

Citizens should describe the problem. Janavani should carry the complexity of understanding systems, pathways, and relevant information.

The citizen remains the decision-maker. Janavani reduces procedural friction while preserving citizen agency.

The Janavani Pathway

From citizen voice to a structured civic pathway.

A clear sequence turns a concern into an understandable civic process.

01

Describe

Trust & Boundaries

Clear boundaries are part of trustworthy civic infrastructure.

Not a Government Authority

Janavani does not become the government, an official department, or an official decision-maker.

Not a Substitute for Official Decisions

Information and assistance do not replace lawful decisions by competent authorities or qualified professionals.

Not an AI Authority

AI can assist with information and pathways; it should not decide what a citizen must do.

The Janavani Principle

Public systems belong to the public.

Janavani is an attempt to make that relationship more understandable, more actionable, and more accountable.
