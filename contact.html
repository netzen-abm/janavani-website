Join the Janavani ecosystem.

Janavani is being built as civic infrastructure for citizen voice. People can participate in different ways: by using the system, contributing knowledge, improving the technology, documenting what they learn, or helping make civic information more understandable.

Participation should increase citizen agency.

Get involved in ways that are clear, voluntary, and proportionate. The presence of a platform or contribution category does not by itself create a formal programme, membership, employment opportunity, grant, or institutional partnership.

03 Â· Contact safety

Contact Janavani without oversharing.

A request for help or collaboration should contain enough context to understand the purpose, but not unnecessary sensitive information.

WHAT TO INCLUDE

Make the purpose clear.

  • What you are trying to understand, improve, research, or contribute.
  • Relevant public context or non-sensitive supporting information.
  • The type of participation you are interested in.
  • Specific questions or proposed next steps where useful.

WHAT NOT TO SEND UNNECESSARILY

Protect personal information.

  • Aadhaar numbers or unnecessary government-identification data.
  • Passwords, authentication codes, or financial credentials.
  • Private medical information.
  • Other sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the purpose.
