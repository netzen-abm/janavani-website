There Is More Than One Way to Participate

Democracy is not limited to elections or protest. Citizens can participate by asking questions, requesting information, making representations, submitting objections, documenting problems, supporting public-interest work and using lawful administrative and judicial processes.

Janavani aims to make these pathways easier to understand and easier to use.

CITIZEN TOOL

JanaVaniBot — Telegram

JanaVaniBot is a citizen-facing tool through which people can describe a government-related problem, raise a concern and move through a structured civic workflow.

It can help citizens organise the information needed to understand an issue, identify a possible government pathway and work toward an appropriate government-facing document.

Telegram is one interface to Janavani. The broader Janavani platform is being developed so that citizens can access its civic capabilities through different interfaces over time.

Lawful Civic Action

When citizens disagree with a government decision, policy or administrative action, protest is not the only possible response.

Depending on the circumstances, citizens may also communicate through written representations, objections, complaints, information requests, legal notices, petitions and other lawful procedures.

Citizens may also choose to represent themselves before appropriate authorities or courts where the applicable rules permit it.

A party-in-person approach can sometimes provide a pathway for people who cannot or do not wish to engage a private lawyer.

Important

Janavani does not provide legal representation and does not guarantee that a particular legal, administrative or judicial pathway will succeed.

Citizens should verify applicable procedure, jurisdiction, deadlines and legal requirements before taking action.

Please Protect Your Own Privacy

This public website is not intended to collect sensitive information about your government dispute, medical records, identity documents, financial information or other private matters.

Do not publish sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues, discussions or other public channels.

When Janavani's citizen-service workflows become available, use the appropriate private workflow rather than publishing sensitive information publicly.

From Users to Co-Creators

Janavani's long-term direction is not simply to build another complaint website.

The goal is to progressively create infrastructure through which citizens can understand government, document their experiences, participate in public decision-making, provide evidence-based feedback and contribute to more accountable governance.
