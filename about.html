About Janavani

Making Government Accountable. One Complaint at a Time.

Our Mission

Empower every citizen in India with the tools and knowledge to hold government accountable through legally sound complaints backed by the Constitution and applicable laws.

Our Vision

A India where every citizen can easily report government misconduct, access their rights, and get instant legal support without fear, cost, or complexity.

Our Values

Transparency, Privacy, Justice, Accessibility, and Empowerment. We believe in open-source solutions and decentralized technology.

The Story Behind Janavani

Janavani means "Voice of the People" in Sanskrit. The idea was born from a simple observation:

Millions of citizens visit government offices daily - hospitals, police stations, panchayats, MVD - and face delays, misbehavior, and denial of services. Yet most don't know:

  • Which law applies to their situation
  • How to file a formal complaint
  • What compensation they deserve
  • Whom to address their complaint to

Janavani solves this by combining:

  • 🇮🇳 Constitutional Knowledge - Backed by Constitution of India and 5 major laws
  • 🤖 AI Technology - Instant legal complaint generation using LangChain & SLMs
  • 🔒 Privacy First - Decentralized (Freenet + Nostr), 100% anonymous, no tracking
  • 🆓 Free & Open - No login, no fees, fully open-source

Key Features

01

Rate & Report

Simple, intuitive interface to report your experience at any government office. Rate 1-5 stars and describe what happened.

02

Legal Brain

AI-powered system that analyzes your complaint and automatically suggests the applicable laws from Indian Constitution and statutory acts.

03

Auto PDF

Download a professionally formatted legal complaint letter with correct authority addresses, proper CC recipients, and signatures.

Laws We Reference

Constitution of India

Articles 14, 16, 19, 21 - Right to Equality, Liberty, and Life

Bhartiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)

Evidence Act for documenting complaints and establishing facts legally

Information Technology Act, 2000

Sections 66, 67 - Protection against harassment and cyber offenses

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Rights when government services are treated as consumer services

Indian Contract Act, 1872

Section 43 - Government obligations to citizens

Your right to ask for information and government transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Janavani really anonymous?

Yes! We don't require login, don't store personal data on central servers, and use Freenet + Nostr for privacy. Your complaint is yours alone.

Do I need a lawyer?

No. Janavani generates legal complaints based on actual Indian laws. The complaint is formatted professionally and ready to send.

Is it free?

Completely free. No hidden fees, no premium features. We believe justice shouldn't be behind a paywall.

Which government offices are supported?

Currently: Hospitals, Panchayat, Motor Vehicle Department, Traffic Police, Ration Shops, Police Stations. More offices coming soon.

Can I contribute?

Yes! Check our GitHub repository: github.com/netzen-abm/janavani. We welcome developers, lawyers, and designers.

How is this different from other complaint platforms?

We combine legal AI, constitutional backing, privacy-first design, and open-source code. No corporate tracking, no centralized databases.
