What is Janavani?

The Infrastructure of Citizen Voice

Janavani is a citizen-centered civic technology initiative designed to help people navigate public problems and participate more effectively in accountable governance.

Janavani assists citizens. It does not become the citizen's authority.

02 Â· Identity

A civic layer between citizens and public systems.

The website is a public-facing brochure, showcase, and discovery layer. It is not the Janavani application itself.

03 Â· Mission

Make civic participation easier, clearer, and more accessible.

Janavani helps citizens understand government systems, identify appropriate pathways, prepare for action, and retain control over their own civic voice.

06 Â· Boundaries

What Janavani is not.

Clear boundaries are part of trustworthy civic infrastructure.

Not a government department

Janavani does not make governmental decisions, issue official determinations, or represent itself as a government authority.

Not a political party

Janavani is a civic technology initiative, not an organisation seeking political power through party activity.

Not a court or legal authority

Information provided by Janavani does not replace lawful institutional procedures or qualified professional advice.

Not merely a Telegram bot

Telegram is an interface. It does not define the full identity, architecture, or long-term purpose of Janavani.

Not an AI authority

AI assistance must remain reviewable and bounded. Generated material must not be treated as unquestionable truth or official determination.

Not a guarantee of outcomes

Janavani must not guarantee government response, legal correctness, successful communication, institutional action, or resolution of every civic problem.

07 Â· Trust

Evidence, privacy, and human review are part of the design.

Privacy and AI claims must remain proportional to the controls and capabilities actually implemented. Planning material is not proof of deployment.
