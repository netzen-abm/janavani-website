Government Should Work for the People.

Janavani is a privacy-first citizen governance platform designed to help people understand government processes, take informed civic action and participate in public accountability.

How Janavani Works View the Project

What is Janavani?

Janavani means the voice of the people.

Janavani is being built as a citizen-governance platform that helps people move from a government-related problem to informed and documented civic action.

Instead of expecting citizens to understand complicated government structures, departments, offices and procedures, Janavani aims to carry that complexity for them.

What Janavani Can Do

Describe a Problem

Explain your government-related problem in simple language.

Identify the Right Action

Janavani helps structure the problem and identify the appropriate civic document or workflow.

Find the Relevant Office

Identify the appropriate government office based on the information available to the platform.

Generate Documents

Prepare structured government-facing documents that citizens can review before using them.

Privacy First

Janavani is designed around data minimisation, privacy and citizen control.

Open Source

The project is being developed openly so that others can inspect, improve and contribute to it.

Where Janavani Is Going

From civic assistance to participatory governance.

Government Performance

Help citizens understand public performance, budgets, commitments and outcomes.

Elected Representatives

Enable citizens to access and understand publicly available information about the work of MPs, MLAs and local representatives.

Government Offices

Enable structured citizen feedback about public services, accessibility and service experience.

Evidence & Follow-up

Support documented complaints, evidence, tracking and appropriate escalation.

Public Accountability

Build reliable public information about government performance using verified information.

Citizen Participation

Move from citizens as users of government services toward citizens as informed participants in governance.

Our Principle

Democracy does not end with voting.

Citizens need practical ways to understand government, exercise their rights, provide evidence, participate in public decisions and hold institutions accountable.

Janavani is being built to make that participation easier, safer and more accessible.
