# 3. FOUNDATIONAL IDENTITY

## VISION

A society in which every citizen has the practical capacity to
understand, participate in and influence the systems through which
they are governed.

## MISSION

To make civic participation easier, clearer and more accessible by
helping citizens understand government systems, identify appropriate
pathways, prepare for action and retain control over their own civic
voice.

## PHILOSOPHY

Citizens already possess political agency. What they often lack is
accessible infrastructure through which to exercise it effectively.

## GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Janavani must increase citizen agency, never replace it.

## CORE VALUES

1. Citizen Agency
2. Truth & Verification
3. Privacy
4. Accessibility
5. Accountability
6. Participation
7. Open & Community-Oriented Infrastructure

# 6. MANIFESTO

Janavani believes:

1. Citizenship does not end with voting.
2. Citizens should be able to understand the systems that govern them.
3. Government complexity should not become a barrier to civic action.
4. Technology should reduce institutional complexity.
5. AI should assist citizen judgement, not replace it.
6. Privacy is a condition of meaningful civic participation.
7. Evidence must be distinguished from allegation and opinion.
8. Accountability requires documentation, verification and follow-through.
9. Participation should become a normal part of democratic life.
10. Better governance requires stronger citizen capacity.

# 10. WHAT JANAVANI IS NOT

Janavani is not:

- a government department;
- a political party;
- a court;
- a replacement for official procedures;
- a substitute for professional legal advice;
- an authority that decides what citizens should believe;
- an AI authority that replaces citizen judgement.
