# 4. Q&A 01

Question:

What is Janavani?

Answer:

Janavani is civic infrastructure being built to help citizens
understand public systems, identify appropriate pathways,
prepare for civic action and participate more meaningfully
in governance.

Janavani is designed to increase citizen agency rather than
replace it.

---

# 5. Q&A 02

Question:

What problem does Janavani address?

Answer:

Janavani addresses the distance between a citizen's concern
and meaningful civic action.

Government systems, procedures, authorities and evidence can
be difficult to understand or navigate.

Janavani seeks to reduce that complexity by helping citizens
move from concern to understanding, appropriate authority,
prepared action and follow-up.

---

# 6. Q&A 03

Question:

Who is Janavani for?

Answer:

Janavani is intended for citizens who want to understand,
navigate and participate more effectively in the public systems
that affect their lives.

It may also become useful to researchers, developers,
journalists, students, civic organisations and others who
contribute to civic knowledge and infrastructure.

---

# 7. Q&A 04

Question:

What does Janavani do?

Answer:

Janavani helps citizens organise a civic concern into a clearer
path toward action.

Depending on the capability currently available, this can
include helping a citizen describe a problem, understand the
relevant context, identify an appropriate pathway and prepare
a civic document.

Current functionality must always be distinguished from
capabilities under development.

---

# 8. Q&A 05

Question:

Does Janavani replace government procedures?

Answer:

No.

Janavani does not replace official government procedures,
authorities or legal processes.

It is intended to help citizens understand and navigate those
systems more effectively.

Citizens remain responsible for reviewing information and
using the appropriate official channel.

---

# 9. Q&A 06

Question:

Does Janavani replace citizen judgement?

Answer:

No.

Janavani's governing principle is:

Janavani must increase citizen agency, never replace it.

The citizen remains the decision-maker.

Technology and AI may assist with understanding, organisation
and preparation, but meaningful civic action should remain
under citizen control.

---

# 10. Q&A 07

Question:

Is Janavani an AI authority?

Answer:

No.

Janavani is not intended to become an authority that decides
what citizens should believe or do.

AI may be used as an assistance layer within the infrastructure,
but it should remain accountable to the citizen and should not
replace human judgement.

---

# 11. Q&A 08

Question:

What does Janavani do now?

Answer:

The current Janavani MVP is focused on building and verifying
a practical citizen journey through its available interfaces.

Current capability should always be described using the
NOW / BUILDING / FUTURE distinction.

Only verified functionality should be presented as available
today.

---

# 12. Q&A 09

Question:

What is Janavani building?

Answer:

Janavani is being developed incrementally toward broader
citizen guidance, civic action, evidence organisation,
follow-up and accountability infrastructure.

These capabilities are under development and must not be
presented as fully operational unless they have been verified.

---

# 13. Q&A 10

Question:

What is Janavani's long-term direction?

Answer:

Janavani's long-term direction is to progressively reduce the
distance between citizens and the systems through which public
decisions are made.

The broader progression is:

Citizen Voice
→ Understanding
→ Evidence
→ Civic Intelligence
→ Participation
→ Accountability
→ Better Governance

This represents long-term direction rather than a claim of
current functionality.

---

# 14. Q&A 11

Question:

How does Janavani approach privacy?

Answer:

Privacy is treated as a condition of meaningful civic
participation.

Janavani's design direction is to minimise unnecessary exposure
of citizen information and to develop civic infrastructure with
privacy-conscious principles.

Specific privacy practices should always reflect the actual
technical implementation.

---

# 15. Q&A 12

Question:

How can I participate in Janavani?

Answer:

People can participate by using the available workflows,
testing the system, reporting problems, contributing research,
improving documentation, contributing technical work, sharing
civic knowledge or helping improve accessibility.

Participation should help strengthen the infrastructure itself.
