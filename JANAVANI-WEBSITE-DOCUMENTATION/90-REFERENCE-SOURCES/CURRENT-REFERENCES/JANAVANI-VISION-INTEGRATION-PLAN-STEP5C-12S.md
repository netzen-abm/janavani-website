# 4. HERO

Eyebrow:

WHY JANAVANI

Headline:

Democracy becomes stronger when citizens can participate in how they are governed.

Supporting statement:

Janavani is being built as civic infrastructure that helps citizens
understand public systems, exercise their existing agency and
participate more meaningfully in governance.

Primary CTA:

Explore How It Works

Secondary CTA:

Get Involved

---

# 5. WHY JANAVANI EXISTS

Core proposition:

Citizens already possess political agency.

What many citizens lack is accessible infrastructure through which that
agency can be exercised effectively.

Preserve the explanation of:

- government complexity;
- difficult procedures;
- difficulty identifying authorities;
- difficulty organising evidence;
- difficulty translating concerns into meaningful action.

Core closing:

Janavani does not seek to speak instead of citizens.

It seeks to help citizens speak with greater clarity, evidence and agency.

# 7. VISION

Primary statement:

A society in which every citizen has the practical capacity to
understand, participate in and influence the systems through which they
are governed.

Supporting principle:

Participation should not be limited to elections.

It should become part of ordinary democratic life.

---

# 8. MISSION

Use:

To make civic participation easier, clearer and more accessible by
helping citizens understand government systems, identify appropriate
pathways, prepare for action and retain control over their own civic voice.

---

# 9. PHILOSOPHY

Core ideas:

Citizens already possess political agency.

Janavani treats citizen participation as infrastructure.

Technology should reduce unnecessary complexity without removing human
judgement.

AI should assist citizens rather than become an authority over them.

The citizen remains the decision-maker.

---

# 10. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Primary statement:

Janavani must increase citizen agency, never replace it.

Supporting principles:

- citizen reviews and approves meaningful civic action;
- Janavani assists rather than commands;
- evidence remains distinguishable from allegation and opinion;
- technology remains accountable to the citizen;
- privacy is a condition of meaningful participation;
- future capabilities are never presented as current capabilities.

---
