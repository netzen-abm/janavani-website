# 3. WHY JANAVANI EXISTS

Headline:

Why does Janavani exist?

Core proposition:

Citizens already possess political agency.

What many citizens lack is accessible infrastructure through which that agency can be exercised effectively.

Government systems can be difficult to understand.

Procedures can be difficult to locate.

Authorities can be difficult to identify.

Evidence can be difficult to organise.

And legitimate civic concerns can become difficult to translate into meaningful action.

Janavani exists to reduce those barriers.

It does not seek to speak instead of citizens.

It seeks to help citizens speak with greater clarity, evidence and agency.

# 4. THE DEMOCRATIC GAP

Headline:

The gap is not only political. It is infrastructural.

Explain that democracy requires more than periodic participation.

Citizens need practical capacity to:

- understand;
- question;

- verify;
- communicate;
- participate;
- follow up;

The problem Janavani addresses is therefore not simply lack of information.

↓

# 6. OUR MISSION

Headline:

Our Mission

Mission statement:

To make civic participation easier, clearer and more accessible by helping citizens understand government systems, identify appropriate pathways, prepare for action and retain control over their own civic voice.

---

# 7. OUR PHILOSOPHY

Headline:

Our Philosophy

Core statement:

Citizens already possess political agency.

What they often lack is accessible infrastructure through which to exercise it effectively.

Janavani therefore treats citizen participation as infrastructure.

Technology should reduce unnecessary complexity without removing human judgement.

AI should assist citizens rather than become an authority over them.

The citizen remains the decision-maker.

---

# 8. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Headline:

Our Governing Principle

Primary statement:

Janavani must increase citizen agency, never replace it.

Supporting principles:

- The citizen reviews and approves meaningful civic action.
- Janavani assists rather than commands.
- Evidence must remain distinguishable from allegation and opinion.
- Technology must remain accountable to the citizen.
- Privacy is a condition of meaningful participation.
- Future capabilities must never be presented as current capabilities.

---

# 9. CORE VALUES

Headline:

The Values That Govern Janavani

1. CITIZEN AGENCY

Citizens retain ownership of their civic voice and decisions.

2. TRUTH & VERIFICATION

Evidence, allegation, interpretation and opinion must not be presented as the same thing.

3. PRIVACY

Citizen participation should not require unnecessary exposure of personal information.

4. ACCESSIBILITY

Civic infrastructure should reduce barriers created by technical, administrative or procedural complexity.

5. ACCOUNTABILITY

Meaningful participation requires documentation, follow-through and institutional responsiveness.

6. PARTICIPATION

Democracy should enable citizens to participate beyond periodic elections.

7. OPEN & COMMUNITY-ORIENTED INFRASTRUCTURE

Long-term civic infrastructure should favour transparency, interoperability and meaningful community participation where appropriate.
