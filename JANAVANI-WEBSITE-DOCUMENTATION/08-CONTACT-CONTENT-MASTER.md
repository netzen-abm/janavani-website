**# JANAVANI — CONTACT / GET INVOLVED CONTENT MASTER**

**\*\*Status:\*\*** Draft — Website Content Authority  

**\*\*Version:\*\*** 1.0  

**\*\*Date:\*\*** 26 August 2026  

**\*\*Page:\*\*** Contact / Get Involved (\`contact.html\`)  

**\*\*Purpose:\*\*** Define the authoritative editorial, structural, participation, boundary, safety, SEO, accessibility, and implementation content for the Janavani Contact / Get Involved page.

**---**

**# 1. PURPOSE**

The Contact / Get Involved page explains how people can connect with Janavani, follow the project, contribute where appropriate, and identify the correct channel for communication.

The page should be practical and action-oriented.

It must not become a duplicate of Home, About, Vision, Resources, or How It Works.

The governing principle is:

\> **\*\*Make participation clear without implying commitments, programmes, partnerships, or institutional authority that have not been verified.\*\***

**---**

**# 2. CONTACT / GET INVOLVED PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY**

The page answers:

\> **\*\*How can I connect with or participate in Janavani?\*\***

It should help visitors understand:

\- how to contact the project;

\- where to follow project development;

\- where appropriate public/community channels exist;

\- how technical contributors may participate;

\- how researchers, writers, designers, civic researchers, and other contributors may engage;

\- what information should be included when contacting the project;

\- and which channels are informational, social, technical, or contribution-oriented.

The page should make the next action obvious without creating unnecessary friction.

**---**

**# 3. CONTACT PRINCIPLE**

Contact information is part of civic trust.

The page must therefore:

\- identify channels clearly;

\- use verified links only;

\- distinguish official project channels from external/community platforms;

\- avoid dead or placeholder links;

\- avoid implying that every channel is actively monitored;

\- avoid promising response times unless one is actually established;

\- avoid implying government affiliation;

\- and preserve citizen agency.

**---**

**# 4. WHAT CONTACT / GET INVOLVED IS FOR**

The page may support the following purposes:

**### General contact**

Questions about Janavani, its purpose, public website, or project direction.

**### Technical contribution**

Developers and technical contributors interested in the open project.

**### Design and content contribution**

Designers, writers, accessibility contributors, researchers, and documentation contributors.

**### Civic / legal research**

People who can contribute verified civic, constitutional, institutional, or legal research.

**### Community participation**

People interested in following, discussing, testing, or helping the project develop.

**### Project feedback**

Useful observations about the website, accessibility, content clarity, links, or public-facing information.

**---**

**# 5. WHO CAN PARTICIPATE**

Potential contribution categories include:

\- Developers

\- Designers

\- Researchers

\- Legal / civic researchers

\- Writers

\- Students

\- Journalists

\- Volunteers

\- Civil-society organisations

\- Accessibility contributors

\- Documentation contributors

\- Community members

These categories describe possible forms of participation.

They must not be presented as a formal programme, employment opportunity, membership scheme, grant programme, or institutional partnership unless such a programme is independently verified.

**---**

**# 6. CONTRIBUTION PRINCIPLE**

Janavani is an open-development project in the sense established by its project documentation.

However:

\> **\*\*Open development does not mean that every contribution, proposal, or external relationship is automatically accepted or endorsed.\*\***

Contributions should be evaluated according to:

\- relevance;

\- factual reliability;

\- civic usefulness;

\- safety;

\- privacy;

\- accessibility;

\- legal and ethical considerations;

\- project principles;

\- and implementation feasibility.

**---**

**# 7. CONTACT CHANNELS**

The page should use only currently verified contact and social channels. It must maintain verified contact channels and verified social channels, with no invented links, no invented partnerships, no unsupported response guarantees, and no unsupported government-affiliation claims.

Known project ecosystem references may include:

\- GitHub

\- Instagram

\- Facebook

\- Bluesky

\- Primal

\- Njump.Me

\- Nostria

\- Telegram

Each channel must be independently verified before publication.

The page must distinguish:

\`\`\`text

OFFICIAL PROJECT CHANNEL

        ↓

COMMUNITY / SOCIAL CHANNEL

        ↓

EXTERNAL REFERENCE

\`\`\`

A platform appearing in the Janavani ecosystem does not by itself establish a formal partnership with that platform. No invented links or invented partnerships are permitted, and no unsupported response guarantees or unsupported government-affiliation claims may be made.

**---**

**# 8. GITHUB / OPEN DEVELOPMENT**

Where the verified Janavani GitHub repository is linked, the page may explain that it is the primary public location for technical development and repository-level collaboration.

The page should not claim:

\- a contribution programme that does not exist;

\- a formal governance structure that is not implemented;

\- guaranteed issue response;

\- guaranteed pull-request acceptance;

\- or institutional sponsorship.

Repository links must be verified before publication.

**---**

**# 9. TELEGRAM AND OTHER INTERFACES**

Telegram may be described as a Janavani interface where the implementation is verified.

The page must preserve the established architectural boundary:

\`\`\`text

JANAVANI WEBSITE

Explain + discover + educate + connect

        ↓

WEB APP / TELEGRAM / OTHER INTERFACES

Citizen action

        ↓

SHARED JANAVANI PLATFORM

Services + workflow + intelligence + storage

\`\`\`

The Contact page must not imply that the website itself performs application workflows that belong to the Web App or Telegram interface.

Future interfaces must be labelled as:

**\*\*BUILDING\*\*** or **\*\*FUTURE / RESEARCH\*\***

unless independently verified as current.

**---**

**# 10. WHAT TO INCLUDE WHEN CONTACTING JANAVANI**

Where appropriate, visitors should be encouraged to provide:

\- the purpose of the message;

\- relevant context;

\- links or references when reporting an issue;

\- screenshots or other evidence where useful;

\- the specific page or feature concerned;

\- and the desired outcome, if applicable.

Visitors should avoid unnecessarily submitting sensitive personal information.

**---**

**# 11. PRIVACY AND CONTACT SAFETY**

The Contact page must not encourage unnecessary disclosure of:

\- Aadhaar numbers;

\- passwords;

\- financial credentials;

\- authentication codes;

\- private medical information;

\- unnecessary government-identification data;

\- or other sensitive personal information.

If a matter requires sensitive information, the page should direct the visitor to an appropriate verified secure channel rather than requesting sensitive information through an ordinary public contact mechanism.

Contact does not equal confidentiality.

The page must not promise absolute confidentiality unless such protection is actually implemented and documented.

**---**

**# 12. CITIZEN AGENCY**

Contact with Janavani does not transfer civic authority to Janavani.

The governing relationship remains:

\`\`\`text

JANAVANI ASSISTS

       ↓

CITIZEN REVIEWS

       ↓

CITIZEN DECIDES

       ↓

CITIZEN ACTS

\`\`\`

Janavani may help explain, organise, connect, or communicate.

It does not make official determinations on behalf of government institutions.

**---**

**# 13. AI POSITION**

AI assistance may support Janavani's development or citizen-facing capabilities where implemented.

The Contact page must preserve the distinction:

\`\`\`text

AI ASSISTANCE

      ≠

OFFICIAL DETERMINATION

\`\`\`

AI-generated or AI-assisted material must not be presented as an official government decision, legal determination, or guaranteed institutional outcome.

**---**

**# 14. CURRENT / BUILDING / FUTURE**

All contact-related capabilities and channels must be classified as:

\`\`\`text

CURRENT

BUILDING

FUTURE / RESEARCH

\`\`\`

Only verified capabilities may be described as current.

Future possibilities must never be presented as current implementation without verification.

Planning material is not proof of deployment.

**# 17. VISUAL CONTENT PRINCIPLE**

The Contact page should be visually simple and trustworthy.

Useful visuals may include:

\- verified social/contact channel icons;

\- contribution pathways;

\- a concise participation diagram;

\- repository/community relationship diagrams where useful.

Avoid:

\- decorative contact graphics;

\- fake statistics;

\- unsupported community-size claims;

\- fake testimonials;

\- artificial urgency;

\- or visuals implying institutional partnerships.

Every visual must explain something useful or make an action easier to understand.

**---**

**# 18. SEO CONTENT REQUIREMENTS**

The page should provide clear answers to useful search questions such as:

\- How can I contact Janavani?

\- How can I contribute to Janavani?

\- Where can I follow Janavani?

\- Can I contribute to the Janavani project?

\- Where is the Janavani source code?

\- How can I provide feedback about the Janavani website?

The page should use:

\- one clear H1;

\- logical H2/H3 hierarchy;

\- concise direct answers;

\- meaningful internal links;

\- a unique title;

\- a unique meta description;

\- canonical URL;

\- Open Graph metadata;

\- crawlable HTML;

\- descriptive image alt text;

\- and structured data only where it accurately describes visible content.

**---**

**# 19. ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS**

The Contact page must preserve:

\- semantic HTML;

\- logical heading hierarchy;

\- descriptive link text;

\- keyboard-accessible controls;

\- visible focus states;

\- sufficient contrast;

\- readable text;

\- mobile usability;

\- accessible social icons;

\- meaningful alternative text where required;

\- and no information conveyed by colour alone.

Icon-only links must have accessible names.

External links should be identifiable where appropriate.

**---**

**# 20. CLAIM CONTROL**

Janavani must not invent:

\- contact channels;

\- social accounts;

\- partnerships;

\- community programmes;

\- contributor programmes;

\- response guarantees;

\- institutional affiliations;

\- statistics;

\- endorsements;

\- or deployment claims.

External platforms must not be described as Janavani partners unless the relationship is independently verified.

The website must not guarantee:

\- a response;

\- government action;

\- legal correctness;

\- institutional outcomes;

\- or resolution of every citizen problem.

**---**

**# 21. SOURCE AUTHORITY**

The Contact page should be governed by:

1\. Verified Janavani project documentation.

2\. Verified current repository and implementation evidence.

3\. Verified current contact/social URLs.

4\. Established Janavani content architecture and page boundaries.

5\. Authoritative institutional sources where explicitly referenced.

6\. Carefully identified future or research material.

When sources conflict, current verified project evidence takes precedence over older drafts.
