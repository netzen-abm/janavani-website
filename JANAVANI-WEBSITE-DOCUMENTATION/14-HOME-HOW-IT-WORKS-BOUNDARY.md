# JANAVANI — 14-HOME-HOW-IT-WORKS-BOUNDARY

## 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the boundary between the HOME and HOW IT WORKS pages of the Janavani website.

The purpose is to prevent duplication, clarify each page's responsibility, preserve the citizen journey, and ensure that current, building, and future claims remain accurately separated.

This is a content and implementation boundary specification. It does not certify any particular website implementation as complete.

---

## 2. CORE PAGE DISTINCTION

The two pages serve different primary questions.

**HOME answers:**

> What is Janavani, why does it matter, and what should a citizen understand first?

**HOW IT WORKS answers:**

> How does the Janavani civic journey work?

HOME establishes orientation, purpose, trust, and first-step understanding.

HOW IT WORKS explains the process, sequence, interaction model, and boundaries of assistance.

Neither page should absorb the primary responsibility of the other.

---

## 3. HOME RESPONSIBILITY

HOME should establish the public-facing introduction to Janavani.

Its primary responsibilities are:

- explain what Janavani is;
- explain why Janavani matters;
- establish the citizen-centred purpose;
- communicate the verified current value;
- provide clear pathways into the site;
- introduce relevant current, building, or future distinctions where necessary;
- establish trust without unsupported claims;
- provide accessible navigation to deeper information.

HOME should not attempt to document the complete civic journey in operational detail.

---

## 4. HOW IT WORKS RESPONSIBILITY

HOW IT WORKS should explain the verified Janavani civic journey.

Its primary responsibilities are:

- explain how a citizen interacts with Janavani;
- explain the sequence of relevant steps;
- clarify what Janavani assists with;
- clarify what the citizen reviews and decides;
- clarify where official determination belongs;
- distinguish current workflows from building or future workflows;
- explain important limitations or verification points;
- help citizens understand what happens before taking action.

HOW IT WORKS should not become a second HOME page.

---

## 5. CITIZEN JOURNEY BOUNDARY

The shared conceptual model is:

**JANAVANI ASSISTS**

**CITIZEN REVIEWS**

**CITIZEN DECIDES**

**CITIZEN ACTS**

HOME may introduce this philosophy at a high level.

HOW IT WORKS may explain how this philosophy is expressed through the actual verified workflow.

The workflow must not imply that Janavani makes official determinations on behalf of citizens or institutions.

---

## 6. HOME CONTENT HIERARCHY

HOME should generally follow this conceptual hierarchy:

1. Janavani identity;
2. what Janavani is;
3. why it matters;
4. citizen value;
5. clear first actions or pathways;
6. concise explanation of how the platform helps;
7. links to deeper pages;
8. appropriate trust, status, and accessibility information.

The exact visual implementation belongs to the approved design system.

---

## 7. HOW IT WORKS CONTENT HIERARCHY

HOW IT WORKS should generally follow this conceptual hierarchy:

1. what the citizen starts with;
2. how Janavani assists;
3. what information or pathway is identified;
4. what the citizen reviews;
5. what remains the citizen's decision;
6. where official determination belongs;
7. what action the citizen may take;
8. important limitations or verification points;
9. current versus building/future workflow status where applicable.

The sequence must reflect verified implementation evidence.

---

## 8. DUPLICATION CONTROL

HOME and HOW IT WORKS may share short explanatory language where necessary for comprehension.

However:

- HOME should remain concise and orienting;
- HOW IT WORKS should carry the process explanation;
- long process descriptions should not be duplicated on HOME;
- purpose statements should not be unnecessarily duplicated on HOW IT WORKS;
- shared language must not create contradictory claims.

When the same concept appears on both pages, each page should express it according to its specific responsibility.

---

## 9. CURRENT / BUILDING / FUTURE BOUNDARY

Both pages must follow the NOW / BUILDING / FUTURE specification.

### CURRENT

Only verified current capabilities may be described as available or implemented.

### BUILDING

Capabilities under development, integration, testing, or preparation must be identified appropriately.

### FUTURE / RESEARCH

Prospective, conceptual, or research capabilities must not be presented as current functionality.

Planning material is not proof of deployment.

A polished interface, diagram, screenshot, or prototype is not by itself proof of deployment.

---

## 10. HOME CLAIM CONTROL

HOME must not imply:

- capabilities that are not verified;
- government affiliation that is not verified;
- institutional partnerships that are not verified;
- official determination by Janavani;
- guaranteed government action;
- guaranteed legal correctness;
- guaranteed problem resolution;
- unsupported AI capability;
- unverified active channels.

HOME is the highest-visibility page and therefore requires particularly careful claim control.

---

## 11. HOW IT WORKS CLAIM CONTROL

HOW IT WORKS must not describe an idealised workflow as though it were already deployed.

Process diagrams, screenshots, examples, or demonstrations must be labelled appropriately when they represent:

- concepts;
- prototypes;
- planned workflows;
- future capabilities;
- research directions.

Where a workflow depends on an external institution, government service, API, database, or channel, the relevant integration must be verified before being described as operational.

---

## 12. AI BOUNDARY

If AI assistance is described on either page, the distinction must remain explicit:

**AI ASSISTANCE ≠ OFFICIAL DETERMINATION**

AI may assist with verified functions such as:

- explanation;
- summarisation;
- question structuring;
- information discovery;
- navigation;
- multilingual interaction where verified.

AI must not be presented as:

- a government authority;
- an official decision-maker;
- a substitute for competent institutional determination;
- guaranteed legal advice;
- a guarantee of outcome.

---

## 13. ACCESSIBILITY BOUNDARY

Both pages must support:

- semantic HTML;
- one clear H1;
- logical H2/H3 hierarchy;
- keyboard accessibility;
- visible focus states;
- sufficient contrast;
- descriptive links;
- meaningful alternative text;
- accessible controls;
- mobile usability;
- reduced-motion considerations;
- multilingual considerations where implemented.

Accessibility must not depend only on colour, animation, or visual positioning.

---

## 14. NAVIGATION BOUNDARY

HOME should provide clear routes to:

- HOW IT WORKS;
- RESOURCES;
- VISION;
- ABOUT;
- CONTACT / GET INVOLVED;
- other verified primary actions.

HOW IT WORKS should provide contextually appropriate routes to deeper information, resources, or next actions without replacing the site's global navigation.

Links must point only to verified destinations.

---

## 15. VISUAL BOUNDARY

HOME visuals should support:

- identity;
- orientation;
- trust;
- concise understanding;
- primary action.

HOW IT WORKS visuals should support:

- process comprehension;
- sequence;
- explanation;
- interaction understanding;
- boundaries between assistance and official determination.

Visuals must not communicate capabilities more strongly than the verified implementation supports.

---

## 16. SEO AND CONTENT BOUNDARY

Each page should have a distinct content purpose.

HOME should target the broad identity and purpose of Janavani.

HOW IT WORKS should target the explanatory process and civic journey.

Both should contain crawlable text content.

Each page should have:

- a clear and distinct title;
- one clear H1;
- logical heading hierarchy;
- descriptive internal links;
- meaningful content;
- no unnecessary duplication.

SEO optimisation must not distort the citizen-facing purpose of either page.

---

## 17. MULTILINGUAL BOUNDARY

Translations must preserve the distinction between:

- what Janavani is;
- why it matters;
- how Janavani works;
- what the citizen must review;
- what the citizen decides;
- what belongs to official determination.

Translated text may expand significantly in length.

The layout must accommodate longer translated text without breaking the information hierarchy or hiding important status and safety language.

---

The preferred conceptual handoff is:

What is Janavani?

↓

Why does it matter?

↓

What can I do here?

↓

**HOW IT WORKS**

↓

↓

What do I review?

↓

What do I decide?

↓

What action can I take?

The actual links and actions must be verified against the repository implementation before publication.

---

## 19. IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY

HOME and HOW IT WORKS should be implemented according to:

1. approved content masters;
2. approved design system;
3. verified asset inventory;
4. verified shared infrastructure;
5. verified privacy and citizen-agency principles;
6. verified NOW / BUILDING / FUTURE status;
7. actual repository structure.

The current public website must remain untouched during the rebuild until the appropriate implementation and review gate is passed.

---

## 20. QA CHECKLIST

Before implementation or publication, verify:

- HOME answers what Janavani is and why it matters;
- HOW IT WORKS explains the civic journey;
- page responsibilities are not unnecessarily duplicated;
- current claims are verified;
- building claims are labelled;
- future claims are labelled;
- AI assistance is distinguished from official determination;
- citizen review and decision remain visible;
- external integrations are verified;
- links are verified;
- accessibility requirements are preserved;
- multilingual expansion is accommodated;
- SEO hierarchy is logical;
- visual claims match implementation evidence;
- current public website protection remains in force.

---

## 21. CONTENT STATUS

**Status:** HOME / HOW IT WORKS boundary master specification established.

This document defines the separation and relationship between the two pages.

It does not certify either page's implementation as complete.

---

## 22. FINAL HOME / HOW IT WORKS PRINCIPLE

HOME should orient the citizen.

HOW IT WORKS should explain the journey.

The two pages should work together without competing with one another, while preserving accurate status, citizen agency, accessibility, and the boundary between assistance and official determination.

**JANAVANI ASSISTS**

**CITIZEN REVIEWS**

**CITIZEN DECIDES**

**CITIZEN ACTS**

**Technology is not the purpose of Janavani.**
