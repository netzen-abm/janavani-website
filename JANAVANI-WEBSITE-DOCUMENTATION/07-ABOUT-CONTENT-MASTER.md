# 3. ORIGIN AND WHY JANAVANI EXISTS

## The central observation

Citizens already possess political agency.

What they often lack is accessible infrastructure through which that
agency can be effectively exercised.

A citizen may know that something is wrong and still face difficulty
because public systems can be complex, fragmented, difficult to
navigate, or unclear about the appropriate authority or procedure.

Janavani exists to reduce this practical distance.

The intended direction is:

``` text
CITIZEN CONCERN
        ↓
UNDERSTANDING
        ↓
APPROPRIATE CIVIC PATHWAY
        ↓
PREPARED ACTION
        ↓
PARTICIPATION
        ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
```

Janavani is therefore concerned not merely with providing information,
but with strengthening the citizen's practical capacity to understand
and participate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. WHAT IS JANAVANI?

Janavani is a citizen-centered civic technology initiative designed to
help people navigate public problems and participate more effectively in
accountable governance.

Its public-facing identity is:

> **JANAVANI --- The Infrastructure of Citizen Voice**

Its role is to help connect:

``` text
CITIZEN
   ↓
PUBLIC PROBLEM
   ↓
UNDERSTANDING
   ↓
APPROPRIATE CIVIC PATHWAY
   ↓
LAWFUL ACTION
   ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
```

Janavani assists citizens.

It does not become the citizen's authority.

The website is a public-facing brochure, showcase, and discovery layer.
It is not the Janavani application itself.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. WHAT JANAVANI IS NOT

A clear boundary builds trust.

Janavani is:

-   not a government department;
-   not a political party;
-   not a court or legal authority;
-   not a replacement for official government procedures;
-   not a substitute for qualified legal advice;
-   not merely a Telegram bot;
-   not an AI authority that citizens should treat as unquestionable
    truth;
-   and not a guarantee of institutional outcomes.

Janavani must not imply that it:

-   makes governmental decisions;
-   guarantees a government response;
-   replaces lawful institutions;
-   replaces qualified legal representation;
-   guarantees that generated material is legally correct;
-   or guarantees that an attempted communication is successfully
    delivered.

Citizens should independently verify applicable laws, procedures,
deadlines, office details, and official requirements before taking legal
or administrative action.

# 6. MISSION

## Our Mission

Janavani's mission is to make civic participation easier, clearer, and
more accessible by helping citizens understand government systems,
identify appropriate pathways, prepare for action, and retain control
over their own civic voice.

The mission can be expressed as:

``` text
UNDERSTAND
    ↓
IDENTIFY
    ↓
PREPARE
    ↓
PARTICIPATE
    ↓
FOLLOW UP
```

Janavani may help citizens:

-   describe a concern;
-   understand a public problem;
-   identify relevant information;
-   identify an appropriate authority or pathway;
-   organise information;
-   prepare structured communications or documents;
-   understand available civic options;
-   and proceed toward lawful action.

The citizen remains responsible for reviewing and approving action.

# 8. CITIZEN AGENCY

Citizen agency is foundational.

The relationship is:

``` text
JANAVANI ASSISTS
       ↓
CITIZEN REVIEWS
       ↓
CITIZEN DECIDES
       ↓
CITIZEN ACTS
```

Janavani should increase citizen capability rather than create
dependency.

Technology should support citizen judgment, not silently replace it.

The website must never imply that:

-   an AI model is the final authority;
-   Janavani determines what a citizen should believe;
-   Janavani makes decisions on behalf of citizens;
-   or the citizen can transfer responsibility for civic action to the
    system.

The governing principle is:

> **Janavani must increase citizen agency --- never replace it.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. PRIVACY

Privacy is part of Janavani's architectural identity.

Civic participation should not require unnecessary surrender of personal
information.

The privacy direction includes:

-   Privacy by Design;
-   Privacy by Default;
-   data minimisation;
-   purpose limitation;
-   identity minimisation;
-   user control;
-   evidence protection;
-   access control;
-   retention discipline;
-   provenance;
-   and auditability.

Citizens may provide personal information, evidence, documents,
photographs, location information, communications, or identity-related
information in civic contexts.

The system should therefore minimise unnecessary collection,
centralisation, and exposure of citizen data.

Privacy is not merely a website feature.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. TRANSPARENCY AND VERIFICATION

Trust requires clear distinctions between different kinds of
information.

Janavani should distinguish, where relevant, between:

``` text
OFFICIAL SOURCE
SYSTEM-DERIVED INFORMATION
CITIZEN-PROVIDED INFORMATION
EXPERT ANALYSIS
AI-ASSISTED OUTPUT
UNVERIFIED CLAIM
```

These categories must not silently become interchangeable.

The project should emphasise:

-   evidence;
-   provenance;
-   source transparency;
-   verification;
-   context;
-   uncertainty;
-   and separation of fact from interpretation.

The objective is not simply more information.

It is better civic information.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. LAWFUL CIVIC PARTICIPATION

Janavani supports lawful civic participation.

Participation can include:

-   understanding public systems;
-   communicating concerns;
-   seeking information;
-   preparing representations;
-   participating in public discussion;
-   submitting evidence;
-   providing feedback;
-   monitoring public projects;
-   and pursuing appropriate lawful civic pathways.

Citizen vigilance should not become harassment, political targeting,
misinformation, or vigilantism.

The principle is:

> **Observe carefully. Verify responsibly. Act lawfully.**

Janavani should help transform passive frustration into informed civic
participation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. HUMAN REVIEW AND AI POSITION

AI and automation may assist with functions such as:

-   classification;
-   document processing;
-   retrieval;
-   translation;
-   analysis;
-   drafting;
-   and structured workflows.

AI assistance must remain subordinate to citizen agency.

The website must not imply:

-   AI infallibility;
-   government authority;
-   legal authority;
-   guaranteed outcomes;
-   or a single AI model as the source of truth.

Where AI or automated assistance is used, citizens should be able to
distinguish between verified information, citizen-provided information,
AI-assisted interpretation, and information that remains uncertain.

The citizen reviews and decides before taking action.

Official determination remains with the relevant official institution.

# 14. CURRENT / BUILDING / FUTURE

Every capability must be classified honestly.

## 14.1 CURRENT

Current means verified or currently available.

The present Janavani system includes verified citizen-assistance
capability through the existing Telegram-based complaint workflow.

The Telegram interface is an access point to Janavani capability.

It is not the definition of Janavani itself.

Current availability must always be represented only after verification.

## 14.2 BUILDING

Building means actively being developed.

Examples include:

-   the Janavani Web App;
-   a broader shared citizen journey;
-   stronger civic intelligence;
-   structured web workflows;
-   and improved shared platform capabilities.

## 14.3 FUTURE / RESEARCH

Future means research, roadmap, or long-term direction.

Possible future directions include:

-   additional interfaces;
-   expanded APIs;
-   follow-up and tracking capabilities;
-   broader civic intelligence;
-   distributed or community-oriented infrastructure;
-   privacy-preserving infrastructure;
-   decentralised infrastructure;
-   and other research directions.

Future possibilities must never be presented as current implementation
without verification.

Only verified capabilities may be described as current.

# 15. PHILOSOPHY

## Citizens already possess political agency.

Janavani's philosophy is that citizens should not need to become experts
in every government process before they can begin participating.

Technology should reduce unnecessary friction while preserving the
citizen's role.

The intended relationship is:

``` text
INFORMATION
     ↓
UNDERSTANDING
     ↓
CAPABILITY
     ↓
PARTICIPATION
     ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY
```

The goal is not to create a new political authority.

The goal is to make existing democratic participation mechanisms easier
to understand and use.
