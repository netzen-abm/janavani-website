# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the canonical content responsibility of the Janavani Home page.

The Home page is the primary public introduction to Janavani.

Its job is to answer:

> **What is Janavani, why does it matter, and what does it mean for a citizen?**

The Home page must establish the relationship between:

```text
THE REPUBLIC
      ↓
THE PEOPLE
      ↓
CITIZEN AGENCY
      ↓
UNDERSTANDING
      ↓
LAWFUL ACTION
      ↓
ACCOUNTABILITY

The Home page must introduce this relationship without attempting to contain the entire Janavani ecosystem.
Detailed operational explanation belongs primarily to:
04-HOW-IT-WORKS-CONTENT-MASTER.md
________________________________________
# 2. HOME PAGE PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY
The Home page must:
•	introduce Janavani; 
•	establish its constitutional and civic foundation; 
•	explain the citizen problem; 
•	explain the role of Janavani; 
•	establish citizen agency; 
•	introduce the civic journey; 
•	distinguish current capability from building and future capability; 
•	establish trust through truthful communication; 
•	introduce privacy and safety principles; 
•	provide clear pathways to deeper information. 
The Home page must not become:
•	a complete product manual; 
•	a technical architecture document; 
•	a legal advice page; 
•	a detailed government-procedure directory; 
•	a future-technology showcase; 
•	or a duplicate of How It Works. 
________________________________________
# 3. OPENING PRINCIPLE
The Home page should begin with the constitutional premise:
THE REPUBLIC BEGINS WITH THE PEOPLE
This establishes the conceptual foundation for Janavani.
The constitutional framing:
We, the People of India
should connect the constitutional identity of the Republic with the citizen-centered purpose of Janavani.
The opening must feel civic rather than promotional.
It should establish that citizens are participants in public life and governance.
________________________________________
# 4. PRIMARY HOME MESSAGE
The Home page must clearly communicate:
Janavani helps citizens understand public problems, find appropriate civic pathways, communicate lawfully with public institutions, and participate in accountable governance.
The wording may be refined during implementation for clarity and visual rhythm, but the underlying meaning must remain intact.
Janavani assists citizens.
Janavani does not become the citizen's authority.
________________________________________

The Home page should provide a direct answer.
Canonical concept
Janavani is a citizen-centered civic technology initiative designed to help people navigate public problems and participate more effectively in accountable governance.
It helps connect:

   ↓

   ↓

APPROPRIATE CIVIC PATHWAY
   ↓

   ↓

# 6. THE CITIZEN PROBLEM
The Home page should acknowledge that citizens can encounter difficulty when dealing with public systems.
Examples include:
•	not knowing which office or authority is responsible; 
•	not knowing which procedure may apply; 
•	difficulty understanding public information; 
•	difficulty identifying an appropriate civic pathway; 
•	uncertainty about what evidence or documentation may be relevant; 
•	difficulty communicating clearly with institutions; 
•	lack of visibility into how a public issue should be pursued. 
The Home page should frame these as navigation and participation problems.
It must not imply that Janavani can guarantee institutional outcomes.
________________________________________
# 7. HOW JANAVANI HELPS
The Home page should explain the role of Janavani at a high level.
Janavani may help citizens:
•	understand a public problem; 
•	identify relevant information; 
•	identify an appropriate office or pathway; 
•	organize information; 
•	prepare communications or documents; 
•	understand available civic options; 
•	and proceed toward lawful action.

The distinction is:
JANAVANI
   ↓
ASSISTS
   ↓
CITIZEN
   ↓
REVIEWS
   ↓
DECIDES
   ↓
ACTS

# 9. CURRENT / BUILDING / FUTURE
The Home page must distinguish present capability from development and future direction.
## 9.1 CURRENT
A capability may be described as current only when it is:
1.	implemented; 
2.	verified in the repository; 
3.	and available to users in the relevant deployment. 
Current public claims must therefore be verified against implementation.
The Home page must never use planning documents as proof of current capability.
________________________________________
## 9.2 BUILDING
Capabilities actively under development may be presented as:
•	being built; 
•	currently under development; 
•	part of the current build; 
•	or an area of active development. 
They must not be presented as already operational.
________________________________________
## 9.3 FUTURE / RESEARCH
Long-term or exploratory directions may be presented as future possibilities.
These may include broader civic infrastructure, advanced privacy technologies, decentralized systems, resilient communications, advanced AI infrastructure, governance intelligence, and other strategic directions documented elsewhere in the Janavani knowledge base.
Future capabilities must remain clearly identified as future or research.
________________________________________

•	Privacy by Default; 
•	data minimisation; 
•	purpose limitation; 
•	identity minimisation; 
•	user control; 
•	evidence protection; 
•	provenance; 
•	access control; 
•	retention discipline; 
•	auditability; 
•	and abuse prevention.

12-PRIVACY-AND-CITIZEN-AGENCY.md

# 11. WHAT JANAVANI IS NOT
The Home page should establish important boundaries without becoming defensive.
Janavani is not:
•	a government authority; 
•	a court; 
•	an election authority; 
•	a law-enforcement body; 
•	a replacement for qualified legal representation; 
•	an authority that determines legal rights; 
•	an authority that determines government outcomes; 
•	or a guarantee of government action. 
Janavani assists citizens in navigating civic processes.
